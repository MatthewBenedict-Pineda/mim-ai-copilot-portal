The Challenge Under Pressure

During high-pressure major incidents, Major Incident Managers spend valuable time manually writing executive summaries, business impact statements, and stakeholder updates — all while simultaneously coordinating the bridge call, engaging resolver groups, and tracking timelines. This portal shows how AI can take on the writing so the MIM can focus on driving resolution.

      📝 Post-Incident Report (PIR) Checklist

      Once the incident is resolved, a good PIR closes the loop for the business and prevents repeat incidents. Work through these before you consider the MIM fully done.

      • Confirm start and resolve times for the incident
      • Verify the correct Configuration Item (CI) for the issue
      • Provide resolution notes to the technical lead
      • Confirm the "Owning Group" and inform them of the RCA SLA (15 days for P1, 20 days for P2)
      • Obtain a final business impact statement from the business lead
      • Check the accuracy of Sub-Category and Category
      • Confirm impacted business groups with IT Service Delivery or other relevant parties
      • Are we aware of any changes which may have contributed to the outage or issue?
